Model framework

A two-stage jump model → classifier pipeline, retrained on a rolling window.

1
Market & macro data
SPX, VIX, a bond-market proxy (price levels) · Treasury yields and yield-curve slope from FRED
↓
2
Feature engineering
EWMA mean return, downside deviation & Sortino ratio (5/10/21-day) · yield-curve slope · VIX level · SPX–bond rolling correlation
↓
3
Stage 1 — Statistical jump model
Penalized K-means clusters historical days into bullish / bearish regimes, with a persistence penalty that discourages noisy day-to-day flipping
↓
4
Stage 2 — XGBoost classifier
Learns to predict the jump model's regime label from the feature set, so regimes can be forecast forward rather than only labeled in hindsight
↓
5
Rolling walk-forward retraining
15-year training window, retrained every 6 months, hyperparameters selected on a held-out validation slice — each forecast uses only information available at that point in time
↓
6
Daily forecast → position
Bullish → hold SPX · Bearish → hold cash

See About for what this is (and isn't) meant for, and its known limitations.
